MECH RTS

Destroy the enemy HQ (red, north). Hold the yellow capture points for more income.

Buildtap Scout, Assault or Titan (left). Hold to cancel one. Selecttap a mech; double-tap for all of that type; drag one finger to box-select. Orderstap the ground to go there (they shoot on the way, then hold); A-Move makes them stop to fight; tap an enemy or its HQ to attack. Buildersselect one, pick Wall, Turret or Trench, then tap the ground (or drag a line); turn walls and trenches with ⟲ ⟳, then Build. RepairBuilders mend damaged mechs and turrets near them; or tap Repair, then the mech. Defendselected mechs crouch where they are: harder to hit, even more so in a trench. Cameratwo fingers pan, pinch to zoom, twist to turn; tap the map (bottom right) to jump.

On a phone? Turn on Mobile (top left) for smaller controls, and hold the phone sideways.
